How this little universe works

A simplified, fixed-star simulation: inverse-square gravity, independent planets, and no planet-to-planet pulls. Planet mass does not change these test-particle paths.

Drag from empty space or an existing planet. The arrow sets velocity: longer means faster. The dotted path predicts the next 8 seconds at the current launch velocity. A planet can orbit, hit the star, or escape.

Each crossing of the upward note line plays a harmonious pluck. Keyboard: Tab to a planet, use arrows to nudge its velocity, and Delete to remove it. Pause stops time. Your system’s reduced-motion setting suppresses trails and pulses.
