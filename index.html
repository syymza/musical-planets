How this little universe works

Every body attracts every other body, including the moving star. The small cross marks the center of mass. Mass sliders change these pulls; heavier worlds tug the star visibly.

This is a simplified 2D Newtonian model with softened gravity and exaggerated body sizes. Bodies merge on contact, preserving mass and linear momentum. Orbital angular momentum becomes internal spin. Collisions dissipate kinetic energy; this toy does not model heat or binding energy in detail.

Drag from empty space to set launch velocity. Drag an existing planet to change its velocity. While aiming, time freezes and the dotted path predicts the whole system for up to 3 seconds, ending at a collision. A tap on an existing planet selects it. A tap on empty space gives a circular starting velocity; other bodies can disturb it.

Mass edits, launches and removals change the system externally. Total mass is bounded at 4.2; existing mergers are never clipped. Distant bodies remain gravitationally active, even off screen. Reset brings them home.

Each crossing of the upward line from the moving star plays a note. Tab to a planet, use arrows to nudge its velocity, or Delete to remove it. Reduced-motion preferences suppress trails and pulses. Sound starts with a chime on your first Start listening tap. Resuming or unmuting does not repeat it.
